## Codebase Patterns
- This is a Next.js 14 app with Tailwind CSS; components are in /components, pages in /app
- Tailwind opacity utilities (text-white/60, text-white/30, etc.) are preferred over hardcoded rgba values for consistency
- stepNumColors array pattern: define per-step class arrays outside the component and index into them in the .map() for clean conditional styling
